# excel-toolkit

Small Excel tools that put your data to work. Open one and use it in five minutes.

## Who this is for

Small business owners and managers who run on spreadsheets and don't have time to fight them. You don't need to write code. You need the numbers right on Monday morning.

## What's inside

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `power-query-recipes/` | Short Power Query recipes for the jobs that eat your week. Each one comes with a plain-English explanation. |
| `sample-data/` | Made-up files so you can test every tool before you touch your own data. |

### Power Query recipes

- **Clean a messy export:** trims spaces, fixes numbers and dates stored as text, drops blank and junk rows, and splits combined columns.
- **Combine a folder of monthly files:** points Power Query at a folder, stacks the files into one table, and tags each row with its month.

More will follow as they're built.

## How to use a tool

**Workbooks (.xlsx)**

1. Click the file, then click the download button.
2. Open it in Excel.
3. Go to **Data > Refresh All**.

**Recipes**

1. Open the recipe and copy the code.
2. In Excel, go to **Data > Get Data > From Other Sources > Blank Query**.
3. Click **Advanced Editor**, paste the code, and click **Done**.

Each recipe's explanation tells you which line to change to point it at your own file.

Power Query is built into Excel 2016 and later, and Microsoft 365. Nothing to install.
